# 🌿 Ferns N Petals (FNP) Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Excel and Power Query**.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and provide an easy-to-use dashboard for monitoring sales performance.

The dashboard analyzes **orders, revenue, products, categories, occasions, cities, monthly trends, and hourly sales performance**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business needs an efficient reporting solution to understand sales performance and identify important sales trends.

The analysis focuses on answering the following business questions:

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders were placed?
- Which occasions generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which products are the top revenue generators?
- Which cities have the highest number of orders?
- How does revenue change month by month?
- What are the peak revenue hours?
- How much time is required to process orders?

---

## 💡 Solution

I developed an Excel-based interactive dashboard using **Power Query and Excel analytics tools**.

The workflow followed in this project was:

**Raw Data → Power Query → Data Cleaning & Transformation → Pivot Tables → KPIs → Charts → Interactive Dashboard**

Power Query was used to prepare and transform the raw data before performing analysis and creating the final dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following analysis:

### 1. Revenue by Occasion
Analyzes revenue generated from different occasions such as:

- Anniversary
- Raksha Bandhan
- All Occasions
- Holi
- Birthday
- Valentine's Day
- Diwali

### 2. Monthly Revenue Trend
Shows how revenue changes across different months and helps identify high-performing and low-performing periods.

### 3. Revenue by Category
Analyzes revenue contribution from different product categories such as:

- Colors
- Soft Toys
- Sweets
- Cake
- Raksha Bandhan
- Plants
- Mugs

### 4. Orders by City
Compares the number of orders across different cities.

### 5. Revenue by Product
Identifies products contributing the highest revenue.

### 6. Hourly Revenue
Analyzes revenue performance across different hours of the day to identify peak sales periods.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Total revenue generated was approximately **₹35.21 lakh** from **1,000 orders**.
- **Anniversary** was the highest-revenue occasion in the dashboard.
- **Colors** generated the highest revenue among the analyzed categories.
- Revenue performance varied significantly across months.
- A small group of products contributed a significant portion of total revenue.
- City-level analysis highlights locations with comparatively higher order volumes.
- Hourly revenue analysis helps identify periods of higher customer purchasing activity.
